HTML Encode / Decode

Convert HTML entities to special characters and vice versa to safely display or store HTML content.

What is HTML Encoding?

HTML encoding replaces special characters like <, >, and & with their corresponding HTML entities, preventing browsers from interpreting them as markup and protecting against cross-site scripting (XSS) attacks.
