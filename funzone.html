FunZone

Party modes with sound. Name Spinner (pick a random winner) and Dice Throw (roll 1-2 real-style dice) - more modes on the way.

Name Spinner

Add up to 10 names, spin the wheel and let luck pick your winner. Runs full screen with sound on mobile and PC.

  • 1Type a name (e.g. Rohit) and tap Add - repeat for up to 10 names.
  • 2Tap the center Play button to spin the wheel.
  • 3The name under the red pointer is your winner. Press Back to exit.
Dice Throw

Roll 1 or 2 dice that tumble and land just like the real thing. Perfect for house rules, board games and settling bets.

  • 1Choose 1 Die or 2 Dice.
  • 2Tap Throw and watch the dice roll.
  • 3Your result appears (totals shown with 2 dice). Press Back to exit.
