FunZone

Party modes with sound. Name Spinner, Dice Throw, Coin Toss, Pick a Number and Finger Draw - all full screen, for parties and games.

Name Spinner

Add up to 10 names, spin the wheel and let luck pick your winner. Runs full screen with sound on mobile and PC.

  • 1Type a name (e.g. Rohit) and tap Add - repeat for up to 10 names.
  • 2Tap the center Play button to spin the wheel.
  • 3The name under the red pointer is your winner. Press Back to exit.
Dice Throw

Roll 1 or 2 dice that tumble and land just like the real thing. Perfect for house rules, board games and settling bets.

  • 1Choose 1 Die or 2 Dice.
  • 2Tap Throw and watch the dice roll.
  • 3Your result appears (totals shown with 2 dice). Press Back to exit.
Coin Toss

A 3D coin that flips for real. Call heads or tails and settle anything the fair way.

  • 1Tap Flip to toss the 3D coin.
  • 2Watch it spin through the air and land.
  • 3Heads or Tails is shown with a running tally. Press Back to exit.

Pick a Number

Set any range - like 1 to 10 or 1 to 100 - and let the machine pick a random winner for you.

  • 1Enter any range (or tap a quick preset).
  • 2Tap Start - the number spins like a slot machine.
  • 3It decelerates and lands on your random number. Press Back to exit.
Finger Draw

Everyone holds one finger on the screen. Ripples travel around every finger, then all vanish except one lucky winner.

  • 1Each player puts one finger on the screen.
  • 2Tap Start Draw - ripples pulse and a 5-second countdown begins.
  • 3All fingers vanish except one - that player wins!
